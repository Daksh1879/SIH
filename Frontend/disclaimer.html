Important Regulatory Notice

LabelSetu is an automated, assistive self-assessment tool designed to support voluntary pre-market verification. It does NOT constitute an official government certification, statutory approval, or legal clearance of any product or packaging label.

This Disclaimer applies to all information, verification reports, automated assessments, and related digital materials generated or made accessible via the LabelSetu portal, operated by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India.

1. Self-Assessment Tool — Not an Official Certification

LabelSetu is provided solely as an advisory self-check mechanism to help manufacturers, packers, importers, and distributors identify potential omissions or oversights in mandatory labeling declarations prior to commercial distribution.

  • A successful or passing result on LabelSetu does not mean that the product packaging has received government certification, license, or statutory endorsement.
  • Verification outputs must not be used, marketed, or presented to customers, retail channels, or statutory inspectors as evidence of regulatory approval.

2. Indicative Results and Image Quality Dependency

The verification engine uses automated optical character recognition (OCR) and text-matching algorithms. Consequently, analytical results are strictly indicative and fundamentally dependent on input media quality:

  • Image Clarity: Low lighting, glare, reflections, motion blur, wrinkles, curvature, shadows, or low image resolution can impair the engine's ability to read declarations accurately.
  • Stylized Fonts & Orientation: Non-standard font styles, highly stylized typography, vertical layouts, multi-panel wraps, or contrasting background patterns may cause valid text to be omitted or misread.
  • Language Variations: While the portal supports multiple Indian languages, complex script rendering or micro-printing may yield false negatives or false positives.

3. No Replacement for Regulatory Inspection

LabelSetu does not replace, supersede, or substitute physical inspections, statutory audits, or official examinations conducted by authorized enforcement authorities, including:

  • Legal Metrology Officers and Inspectors appointed under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
  • Food Safety Officers under the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI).
  • Drug Inspectors and Licensing Authorities under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
  • Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) or any other statutory body.

Compliance status verified through this portal does not confer legal immunity against statutory penalties, compounding proceedings, product seizures, or consumer dispute actions.

4. Limitation of Liability

The Department of Consumer Affairs, the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, and the Government of India, including its officers, employees, and authorized agents, shall NOT be held legally or financially liable for:

  • Any business, commercial, or operational decisions made on the basis of LabelSetu verification reports.
  • Packaging printing costs, manufacturing batches, marketing collateral, or distribution losses incurred due to discrepancies in automated self-checks.
  • Fines, statutory penalties, confiscation of goods, or legal proceedings initiated by regulatory authorities.
  • Errors, omissions, service interruptions, or software malfunctions of the LabelSetu portal.

5. Direct Consultation of Acts and Rules Required

Users, manufacturers, and legal advisors must refer directly to the official gazetted legislation and statutory notifications. Applicable regulatory frameworks include, but are not limited to:

  • The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, as amended.
  • The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
  • The Food Safety and Standards (Packaging and Labelling) Regulations.
  • The Drugs and Cosmetics Rules and respective industry-specific packaging mandates.

Note for Users: In all circumstances where questions arise regarding statutory compliance, packaging dimensions, minimum font sizes, or mandatory bilingual declarations, users are advised to seek formal guidance from the concerned Controller of Legal Metrology or competent legal practitioners.

About This Portal

LabelSetu is a product label compliance verification service designed to assist manufacturers, packers, and sellers in verifying whether their product labels carry all mandatory declarations as required under applicable rules.
