Supported Product Categories

The required declarations differ by product type. Below are the fields checked in each supported category.

🍪 Packaged Food

Basic declarations expected on packaged food items

  • MRP (Maximum Retail Price)
  • Net Quantity
  • Consumer Care Contact

💄 Cosmetics & Personal Care

Declarations expected on cosmetic and personal care items

  • Manufacturer Name & Address
  • MRP (Maximum Retail Price)
  • Net Quantity
  • Country of Origin
  • Consumer Care Contact
  • Manufacturing Date

📦 General FMCG

Broader checklist for everyday packaged goods not covered above

  • Manufacturer Name & Address
  • MRP (Maximum Retail Price)
  • Net Quantity
  • Country of Origin
  • Consumer Care Contact
  • Manufacturing Date
  • Generic Name of Product

Additional categories will be added as their declaration requirements are mapped.

How Verification Works

1

Upload a Photo

Take or upload a clear photograph of the product label and select the applicable product category.

2

Text Extraction

The system reads and extracts the printed text from the label image automatically.

3

Compliance Check

Extracted text is compared against the mandatory declarations for the selected product category.

4

Verification Report

A report is generated showing which declarations are present and which are missing. Results are saved to your dashboard.

About LabelSetu

Background

Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, pre-packaged commodities sold in India are required to carry specific declarations on their labels — including the manufacturer's name and address, MRP, net quantity, country of origin, and other fields depending on the product type.

Non-compliance with these requirements can result in penalties under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009. Despite this, missing or incomplete declarations remain common, often due to oversight during label design or printing.

Purpose of LabelSetu

LabelSetu is a verification tool that allows manufacturers, packers, and sellers to check a product label against the applicable declaration requirements before the product enters the market.

This service is intended for self-assessment purposes. It does not constitute regulatory approval and does not replace any inspection or certification process prescribed under the applicable laws and rules.
